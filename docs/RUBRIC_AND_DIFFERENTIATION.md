## Judgment

The concept is well aligned and could become a strong candidate. 'Very strong' cannot be established before working-prototype quality and domain validation are demonstrated. There is no defensible winning probability or claim of being the strongest entry. Presentation and measurable business value are the largest unproven parts; full coverage of energy/emissions is illustrative until real meters/factors are available.

## Novelty claims that are defensible

Dashboard + RAG + agent workflow is a common architecture and the user supplied a public Microsoft example. Our proposal's distinguishable emphasis is:

1. Every KPI and hypothesis has a file/sheet/range or slide reference.
2. Conflicting units/readings/threshold versions remain visible and block unsafe comparisons.
3. Historical known cause and pre-event hypothesis are explicitly separated, with temporal evidence gates.
4. Asset OFF, full plant shutdown, reported downtime and sampled rate are separate meanings.
5. Actions are closed only after completion evidence and reviewer confirmation; repairing an asset does not close all CAPA automatically.

These are proposed features; Codex must implement them before claiming demonstration. We cannot claim competitors lack them because their entries were not reviewed. Microsoft inspiration should be attributed. A larger number of agents does not itself demonstrate novelty or business benefit.

## What team should say in its deck

- Solution: 'A governed manufacturing decision hub that connects operational KPIs, traceable root-cause hypotheses and verified follow-up actions.'
- Baseline: 'The competition incident register records downtime and both actual and potential loss. These figures indicate historical exposure; we do not equate them with achievable savings.'
- AI: 'Evidence-backed probable cause indication with engineering review; known historical RCA findings are separate from pre-event hypotheses.'
- Utilities: 'Energy/emissions integration and forecasting are illustrated with labeled sample data because utility baseline inputs were not supplied.'
- Impact: 'A pilot will measure investigation effort, citation correctness, engineer usefulness and verified action completion. Financial improvement remains a hypothesis until operational validation.'
